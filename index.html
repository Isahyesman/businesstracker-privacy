Privacy Policy for Business Tracker

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Overview

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Business Tracker. The application operates locally on your mobile device.

2. Information Collection and Local Storage

All financial tracking data, inventory entries, savings goals, and expense logs created in Business Tracker are stored locally on your device using local storage mechanics (AsyncStorage). We do not collect, transmit, upload, or store your application data on external servers.

3. Data Control and Deletion

Because all stored data resides entirely on your device, you maintain complete ownership and control over your information. Clearing the app's cache/data or uninstalling the app will permanently delete all stored data.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or share your application data with third parties. Standard Android/Expo distribution frameworks may process basic diagnostic metadata in accordance with their standard policies.
